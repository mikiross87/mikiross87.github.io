Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Sleevenote has no servers and no account of its own. I don't collect, see or sell any information about you or your collection. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your records and everything you note about them (grades, prices you paid or sold for, valuations, shelf locations, photos, play and cleaning logs, loans) are stored in Sleevenote on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, apart from any copy kept by iCloud sync.

iCloud sync (Premium)

If you have Premium and iCloud sync is on, your collection is stored in your own private iCloud database so your other devices get it too. Apple holds that data under Apple's Privacy Policy; I can't read it.

Discogs

Searching, scanning, looking up a pressing, loading cover art, checking prices and importing your collection all send requests straight from your device to Discogs. Those requests fall under Discogs' Privacy Policy.

  • Prices are fetched live when you look at a record and are never saved.
  • If you link your Discogs account, its sign-in token is kept in your iCloud Keychain, so your other devices are linked too. Your Discogs username and currency are kept on your device. Unlinking removes them.
  • Sleevenote only reads your Discogs collection; it never changes it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to read a record's barcode, on your device. The barcode number is then sent to Discogs to find the release. Photos you add to a record are picked with the system photo picker, so Sleevenote only receives the photos you choose, never your whole library.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Sleevenote only learns whether Premium is active on your Apple Account.

Exports and widgets

An insurance export (Premium) is a file created on your device. It goes only where you send it. Widgets show your collection from your device.

Children

Sleevenote collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: sleevenote.app@icloud.com.
