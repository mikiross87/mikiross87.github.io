Sleevenote

A home for your vinyl collection on iPhone and iPad.

Scan a barcode or search to add a record, then keep track of your copy: sleeve and media grade, what you paid, where it sits on the shelf, photos, plays, cleanings and loans. Each record shows what it's selling for on the Discogs marketplace. Link your Discogs account and Sleevenote imports your collection and suggests prices by condition.

Sleevenote is free for up to 100 tracked records. Premium adds unlimited tracking, iCloud sync, an insurance export, widgets, and themes.

Common questions

Will Sleevenote change my Discogs collection? No. The import only reads it. Records you add or remove in Sleevenote stay in Sleevenote.

Why did a record I deleted come back after an import? The import adds everything in your Discogs collection that isn't already in Sleevenote. To keep a record you've sold, mark it as sold instead of deleting it.

How do I restore Premium on a new device? Open Settings, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel a subscription? In the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad: your name, then Subscriptions, then Sleevenote. Or open Settings in Sleevenote and choose Manage Subscription.
